Need to Know

Still on the fence? These are the questions John hears most often from new clients. If yours isn't here, the first consultation is the perfect place to ask — and it's completely free.

How much money do I need to start working with you?

There is no minimum. John works with clients at all stages — from those just beginning to save, to high-net-worth individuals managing complex portfolios. What matters most is your commitment to improving your financial future.

What does the first consultation cost?

Your first 30-minute discovery call is completely free and carries zero obligation. It's simply a conversation to see if we're a good fit for each other.

How are you compensated?

John operates on a transparent fee-only model. You pay a clear advisory fee — there are no hidden commissions or product-selling incentives. This means advice is always given with your interests first.

How often will we meet?

Most clients meet quarterly for a review, with additional sessions whenever there's a major life or market event. John is also available between sessions via email and phone for time-sensitive questions.

Can you help with business finances too?

Absolutely. John works extensively with business owners on financial planning, tax strategy, profit extraction, and planning for eventual sale or succession.

John Suan is a certified financial advisor with over 5 years of hands-on experience helping individuals, families, and businesses build lasting financial security. After beginning his career at a leading investment firm, John founded his independent practice to offer clients something the big institutions rarely do: undivided attention, transparent advice, and strategies built entirely around their unique circumstances.
